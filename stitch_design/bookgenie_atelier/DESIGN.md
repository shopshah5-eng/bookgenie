## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the understated prestige of high-end independent publishing houses merged with the precision of contemporary algorithmic intelligence. Designed for distinguished authors, boutique publishers, and literary stylists, the interface feels like an immaculate atelier—an expansive, light-flooded workspace where digital craftsmanship meets the tactile gravitas of fine bookmaking.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Editorial Minimalism with Architectural Rigor**. The UI renounces gratuitous decoration, relying instead on razor-sharp proportional geometry, generous negative space, and disciplined typographic rhythm. Pure crisp backgrounds yield total visual dominance to the manuscript and typesetting previews, while subtle champagne-gold and rich umber-slate accents suggest curated luxury without distraction. Micro-interactions are deliberate and crisp, evoking the decisive click of a hot-metal typesetting arm or the tactile turn of heavyweight deckle-edged paper.

## Colors

The palette is deliberately restrained, prioritizing optical purity, high dynamic contrast for long-form reading, and subtle metallic warmth.

* **Base & Surfaces**: Grounded in pure optical white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary canvas and editing sheets, shifting seamlessly into alabaster (`#FAFAFA`) for surrounding studio toolbars and soft vellum (`#F5F5F7`) for global workbenches.
* **Structural Borders**: Architectural hairpins rendered in ultra-subtle cold grays (`#E5E5EA` and `#EAEAEA`) establish boundaries without competing with live typesetting.
* **Ink & Contrast**: The primary ink color is a deep, velvety carbon slate (`#18181B`), softened only where functional hierarchy demands: secondary editorial text at `#52525B` and caption metadata at `#71717A`.
* **Editorial Accents**: The secondary champagne gold (`#C5A059`) and deep burnished bronze (`#996515`) are reserved strictly for high-value AI synthesis states, edition badges, publication-ready milestones, and selected master styles.
* **Interactive States**: Interactive selections leverage soft gold-tinted cream washes (`#FAF8F5`) bounded by hairline gold strokes, avoiding harsh saturated tints.

## Typography

The typography leverages an intentional dialectic: the high-contrast literary romance of **Playfair Display** paired with the unyielding, crisp structural precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

* **Serif Display Hierarchy**: Playfair Display is calibrated with snug line heights and slight negative tracking for dramatic chapter titles, book covers, and section titling. It must never be set in all-caps, with the exception of specific folio numbers or Roman numerals.
* **Sans-Serif Functional System**: Plus Jakarta Sans delivers pristine clarity at dense tool scales (inspection panels, manuscript word counts, galley margins). It handles all structural body copy, metadata labels, and interactive components.
* **Label Caps**: Category indicators, status badges, and book binding dimensions rely on uppercase micro-type (`11px` with `+0.08em` tracking) to replicate traditional letterpress imprints.

## Layout & Spacing

The studio layout operates on an architectural **3-tier modular fixed-fluid hybrid grid**:

* **Desktop Workbench (>= 1280px)**: 
  * Left Navigation / Spine Index: Fixed width of `280px` for table of contents, book parts, and chapter navigation.
  * Central Manuscript Canvas: Fluid central column with strict max-width (`840px`) centered with generous breathing gutters (`margin-desktop: 3rem`) mimicking an open folio.
  * Right Studio Inspector: Fixed width of `360px` for real-time typography metrics, AI generation controls, and export settings.
* **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: Inspector and chapter index collapse into sliding slide-over drawers with translucent backing. The manuscript expands to occupy the central 8-column layout.
* **Mobile (< 768px)**: Strict 4-column layout (`gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`). Primary controls retreat into an anchoring bottom action sheet, leaving the writing viewport totally unobstructed.

Vertical cadence is anchored to an uncompromising **4px/8px baseline rhythm**. Whitespace around editorial content is intentionally oversized (`space-xl` and higher) to induce deep focus and reduce cognitive exhaustion during extensive composition sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews muddy dropshadows, relying instead on **optical planar surfacing, 1px hairline delimiters, and razor-fine ambient diffusion**.

* **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Background workspace in `#F5F5F7` or `#FAFAFA`. Completely unshadowed.
* **Level 1 (Paper Sheet / Manuscript)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface elevated above the workbench using a crisp 1px border (`#E5E5EA`) combined with a whispered architectural shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.02), 0 8px 24px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`.
* **Level 2 (Panels & Tool Docks)**: Floating formatting toolbars and floating context palettes feature ultra-subtle warm ambient diffusion: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px rgba(229, 229, 234, 0.8)`.
* **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Export Overlays)**: Deep focus modals rely on a light scrim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) framed with crisp 1px borders and layered ambient occlusion (`0 20px 48px -12px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Minimalist Architectural Geometry (Level 1)**.

Edges reflect the crisp precision of trimmed paper stocks and physical book covers. Standard inputs, cards, action triggers, and modular sections utilize subtle `0.25rem` (4px) radii. Larger structural frames and floating canvas trays scale up to `0.5rem` (8px). 

Circular or pill-shaped geometries (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for contextual page-number indicators, status dot pulses, and miniature AI prompt token pills. Roundedness must never feel playful or juvenile; it must remain disciplined and tailored.

## Components

### Buttons
* **Primary (Authoritative Slate)**: Solid `#18181B` fill, `#FFFFFF` text in `label-ui`, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a graceful shift to `#27272A`. Active state micro-compresses (`scale: 0.99`).
* **Secondary (Architectural Outline)**: Background transparent or `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E5E5EA`, text `#18181B`. Hover state applies `#FAFAFA` and darkens border to `#D4D4D8`.
* **Tertiary / Subtle (Champagne AI)**: Background `#FAF8F5`, border 1px solid `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`, text `#996515`. Hover subtly strengthens border to `#C5A059` and warms background.
* **Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#52525B`. Hover applies `#F5F5F7` with text `#18181B`.

### Text Inputs & Studio Prompts
* **Form Inputs**: 4px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E5E5EA` border, padded with `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally. Focus state removes default browser rings, applying a 1px border of `#18181B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #18181B`).
* **Manuscript Inline Prompt**: Floating conversational AI inputs rest inside a card bordered with subtle gold foil hairline (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`), rendering suggestion typography in Playfair italic.

### Cards & Book Preview Tiles
* **Galley Card**: Constructed on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#EAEAEA` perimeter. Padding is generous (`space-lg`).
* **Book Spine / Cover Tile**: Employs an optical 3:4 aspect ratio with an interior 1px inner shadow on the spine axis to simulate a physical book hinge. Hover delivers a slight upward translation (`-2px`) and subtle shadow spread.

### Chips & Badges
* **Edition Badges**: Rendered in uppercase `label-caps` with 2px radius or subtle pill shape. Padded at `2px 8px`. 
  * Draft: `#F5F5F7` background, `#71717A` text.
  * AI Enhanced: `#FAF8F5` background, `#996515` text, 1px hairline `#C5A059`.
  * Print-Ready: `#18181B` background, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
* **Checkboxes**: 14px square, 2px radius, 1px solid `#D4D4D8`. Selected state fills `#18181B` displaying an ultra-thin white glyph checkmark.
* **Radio Controls**: 14px circle, 1px solid `#D4D4D8`. Active state features a centered 6px solid dot in `#18181B`.

### Studio-Specific Publishing Components
* **Folio Margin Rulers**: Razor-thin 1px horizontal and vertical guides rendered in `#E5E5EA` with numeric points in `code-spec` (`#A1A1AA`).
* **Typeset Inspector Floating Bar**: Detached horizontal pill-shelf hovering at bottom-center of the active leaf, housing kerning, leading, drop cap toggles, and serif style switches.
